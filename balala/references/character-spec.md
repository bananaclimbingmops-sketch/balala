# Balala Character Specification

Use the selected mode's identity image as the final authority. This text prevents common drift; it does not replace visual referencing.

## Invariants Across All Modes

- Depict the same cheerful yellow monkey mascot named Balala from Banana Climbing / 香蕉攀岩.
- Preserve the oversized round ears, warm peach inner ears, peach muzzle/face patch, brown oval eyes, small orange-red oval nose, and broad friendly toothy grin when the requested emotion permits.
- Preserve the distinctive curved brown banana-shaped stem attached to the top of the head. Follow the selected mode's latest turnaround for its exact silhouette, curvature, thickness, angle, foreshortening, and attachment point. Do not average it with older renders or convert it into hair, a hat, horns, or a floating banana.
- Preserve the long flexible yellow tail and its signature curled gripping tip around a short rounded brown cap whenever the selected mode includes a body. Do not replace it with an ordinary monkey tail, a hand, or a loose banana.
- Keep the palette warm: saturated banana yellow, medium/dark brown details, peach face and ear areas, orange-red nose, and ivory teeth.
- Keep the silhouette round, soft, energetic, child-friendly, and immediately recognizable.
- Allow expression, gaze, pose, props, and activity-specific safety equipment to change.
- Do not add realistic fur, human hair, extra limbs, wings, horns, unrelated logos, or another mascot's traits.

## Controlled Variations

- Clothing: keep canonical clothing by default; allow helmets, harnesses, backpacks, aprons, uniforms, or seasonal accessories only when the activity needs them.
- Props: use at most two by default and make grip/contact physically readable.
- Emotion: retain recognizable ear, face-patch, nose, and head-ornament geometry even during extreme expressions.
- Viewpoint: front or three-quarter view is preferred. Use side/back views only when necessary to show the action.
